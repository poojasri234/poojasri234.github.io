Customer Churn Analysis

An analysis of customer data to understand which characteristics and behaviors are associated with churn, and which segments are most at risk of leaving.

Dataset: Customer account & usage data  •  Tools: Python, SQL, Power BI  •  Focus: Churn Analysis, Segmentation, EDA

1. Business problem

The objective was to understand which customer characteristics and behaviors are associated with churn, so the business can identify at-risk segments earlier and prioritize retention efforts.

2. Business questions

  • What is the overall churn rate?
  • How does churn vary by contract type?
  • How does churn vary by customer tenure?
  • How does churn vary by service type?
  • How does churn vary by payment method?
  • What does the customer tenure distribution look like?
  • How do monthly charges relate to churn?

5. SQL analysis

Purpose: Overall churn rate.

SELECT ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 2) AS churn_rate_pct FROM customers;

Purpose: Churn rate by contract type.

SELECT contract, COUNT(*) AS customers, SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned, ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 2) AS churn_rate_pct FROM customers GROUP BY contract ORDER BY churn_rate_pct DESC;

Purpose: Average tenure of churned vs. retained customers.

SELECT churn, AVG(tenure) AS avg_tenure_months FROM customers GROUP BY churn;
