E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analysis

An end-to-end analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and regional trends using transactional e-commerce data.

Dataset: E-commerce transaction data  •  Tools: SQL, Power BI, Excel  •  Focus: Sales Analysis, Customer Analysis, KPI Reporting

1. Business problem

The objective of this analysis was to understand how the business was performing across products, customers, regions, and time periods. The analysis focused on identifying revenue trends, high-performing product categories, customer purchasing patterns, and opportunities for improving sales performance.

2. Business questions

  • How is revenue changing month over month?
  • Which product categories generate the most revenue?
  • Which products contribute the most to overall sales?
  • Which customer segments generate the highest value?
  • Which regions perform best?
  • What is the average order value?
  • Are there seasonal patterns in purchasing behavior?

  • Created month, quarter, and year fields for trend analysis
  • Prepared aggregated tables for Power BI reporting

5. SQL analysis

Purpose: Analyze monthly revenue trends.

SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', order_date) AS month, SUM(sales) AS total_sales FROM orders GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('month', order_date) ORDER BY month;

Purpose: Identify high-performing product categories.

SELECT category, SUM(sales) AS revenue, SUM(quantity) AS units_sold FROM orders GROUP BY category ORDER BY revenue DESC;

Purpose: Rank top customers by lifetime value.

SELECT customer_id, SUM(sales) AS lifetime_value, COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS orders FROM orders GROUP BY customer_id ORDER BY lifetime_value DESC LIMIT 10;

9. Business recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

  • Prioritize inventory planning around the strongest seasonal sales periods.
  • Evaluate cross-selling opportunities for customers purchasing high-value products.
  • Review low-performing categories to identify pricing or assortment issues.
  • Consider targeted campaigns for repeat customers with high purchase frequency.
