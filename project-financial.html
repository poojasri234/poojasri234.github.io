03 — Financial Performance & Variance Analysis

Business problem

Detect variances faster and explain drivers of financial changes.

Data

Financial ledgers, transaction-level data, and reporting extracts.

Process

Reconciliation → variance decomposition → visualization.

Key findings

  • Identified seasonal drivers responsible for 40% of variance.
  • Found reporting gaps in category mappings.

Recommendation

Standardise category mappings and automate variance alerts for early detection.
