Customer Segmentation & Business Insights

A segmentation-focused analysis of customer transaction and feedback data to identify purchasing patterns, customer segments, and KPI changes.

Dataset: Customer transaction & feedback data  •  Tools: SQL, Python, Power BI  •  Focus: Segmentation, Purchase Behavior, KPI Analysis

1. Business problem

The objective was to understand how customers differ in purchasing behavior and value, so the business can prioritize retention and marketing effort toward the segments that matter most.

2. Business questions

  • How do customers group by purchasing behavior?
  • Which customers purchase most frequently?
  • Which customers generate the most value?
  • What share of customers are repeat buyers?
  • How does purchase behavior trend over time?

4. Data preparation (Python)

Preparation with Pandas included:

  • Handled missing values and inconsistent fields
  • Removed duplicate transaction records
  • Checked and treated basic outliers in order value
  • Built per-customer aggregates (recency, frequency, monetary value)

5. SQL analysis

Purpose: Calculate recency, frequency, and monetary value per customer (RFM).

SELECT customer_id, MAX(order_date) AS last_order_date, COUNT(*) AS frequency, SUM(order_value) AS monetary_value FROM orders GROUP BY customer_id;

Purpose: Rank customers into value tiers using a window function.

SELECT customer_id, monetary_value, NTILE(4) OVER (ORDER BY monetary_value DESC) AS value_tier FROM customer_summary;

Purpose: Identify repeat vs. one-time customers.

SELECT CASE WHEN frequency > 1 THEN 'Repeat' ELSE 'One-time' END AS customer_type, COUNT(*) AS customers FROM customer_summary GROUP BY customer_type;
